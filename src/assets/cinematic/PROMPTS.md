# Origem dos assets

Assets produzidos com a ferramenta de imagem integrada a partir do mockup aprovado. O mockup é uma referência de direção de arte, e o site recompõe a cena com texto e controles reais. Os arquivos WebP são codificações otimizadas dos PNGs gerados, sem alterar a composição. Não são fotografias de um espaço ou de uma escultura reais.

## Escultura — PNG transparente, derivação do mockup

Use case: background-extraction. Input image is the approved website mockup; extract and adapt ONLY its balancing sculpture as a production website animation asset. Output a single large landscape transparent PNG with a genuinely transparent alpha background. No text, no website UI, no floor, no walls, no background shadows. Preserve the beautiful realistic warm travertine stone texture and the antiqued copper/bronze beam and triangular geometric fulcrum, photographic side lighting. Show the ENTIRE sculpture centered with about 8% transparent margin on all sides. Small rough-cut travertine stone on the left, larger travertine stone on the right, resting on a narrow bronze beam with small support pegs like the reference; tall elegant pointed bronze triangular fulcrum beneath the middle of the beam. Very important for animation layer separation: make the beam exactly horizontal, keep ALL beam and stones above a single horizontal line at 54% canvas height, and keep the bronze triangular pedestal BELOW that horizontal line, its sharp tip touching the middle underside of the horizontal beam. No parts of the stand extend above that line; no stones below the beam. Slight perspective depth is fine, but the beam has a perfectly level horizontal lower edge. The complete sculpture stands alone as a precisely isolated photographic cutout, with the same materials and cinematic chiaroscuro as the reference. No extra objects.

## Cenário — derivação do mockup

Use case: precise-object-edit. Input is a website mockup. Create the matching production BACKGROUND image for that website. Remove ALL text, navigation, buttons, lines, interface elements and the ENTIRE bronze and stone sculpture and its shadow. Keep only the beautiful cinematic dark burgundy architectural room: wine stucco walls, subtle recessed architectural planes on the right, a wine floor along the bottom quarter, natural cinematic warm side illumination from upper right, very dark quiet negative space on the left suitable for ivory website text. Preserve the reference's photographic material texture, camera perspective, color palette and moody restrained lighting. Entire wide landscape 16:9 canvas should be a seamless clean photograph of the EMPTY room. No objects, no pillars in foreground, no justice symbols, no bright shapes, no letters, no watermark, no UI. Production-ready cinematic backdrop with subtle floor texture, not an abstract gradient.
